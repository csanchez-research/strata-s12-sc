From the input to the release

Each card is one S1–S2 pair. Strips = where every band comes from: from D_lite (published, copied) added from Google Earth Engine added from Planetary Computer (complete --provider pc) not added: reference check failed, tile kept as published failed (nodata)

Every stratum of the sample (land cover × region or season): verified tiles (COMPLETE) out of all tiles. Click a cell to see its tiles.

Columns:

Median reflectance spectrum of each land-cover class: per band, the median over the verified tiles of the class of each tile's median. B1, B5–B9, B11 and B12 are the bands added by the tool; B2–B4 come from D_lite.

Tiles a curator flagged or annotated (review.json). Marks never change the tiles; flagged pairs are left out of the release by package.
